Primate Locomotion Investigation · End-of-Unit Assessment

Analyze a new specimen

This assessment uses a specimen you have not seen before, generated fresh for this sitting. It is not a memory test — you may use your notes and your completed investigation packet. What matters is whether you can apply the same reasoning to data you have not already discussed with classmates.

Before you begin

This is open-notes, not open-answer. Every question below is about the specific specimen generated for you today — its numbers are different from everyone else's, including your own past assignments. Copying another student's response, or a generic AI-generated answer, will not match your data and will be obvious to your instructor.

Cite specific numbers. Vague claims ("the IMI was high") earn less credit than specific ones ("IMI = 104.2, which falls in the Knuckle-Walker range of 100–120").

Not every display is equally useful for every question. Part of what is being assessed is your judgment about which evidence actually helps answer a given question.
