CAMEL Notebook Catalog

Forty phone-friendly notebooks on chip scaling, 2D materials, and superconductivity, grouped into four rounds. Each one works with no Google account and no runtime — the pictures are already saved and respond to taps. Tap a badge to open it in Colab.

This catalog is the menu a guided session was built from, not itself a guided epiSTEMic investigation — it's archived here for reference. The live notebooks are hosted and maintained in the reinhart-group/camel-2dcc repository; the Colab links below open from there. Local copies of most notebooks are also kept in this folder.
