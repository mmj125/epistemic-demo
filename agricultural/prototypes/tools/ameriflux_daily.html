AmeriFlux daily reducer

Drop an AmeriFlux BASE or FLUXNET zip here. It reads the half-hourly file inside, averages it to one row per day, and gives you a small CSV to commit. Everything happens in this browser tab; nothing is uploaded.

Drop a .zip here or click to choose one
Several zips at once are fine.

Daily mean of every variable present (P is the daily sum); a variable needs 40 percent of the day's records or the cell is blank; -9999 is missing. Units stay as AmeriFlux gives them.
