How much does nitrogen matter?

Runs the selected crop and year at fourteen fertilizer rates (0–650 kg N/ha) back to back, computed on the fly. Yield levels off well before 650 — every kg of N applied past that point still leaches, and past a lower point still, it also costs more than the extra grain is worth.

Rough, adjustable figures for illustrating the tradeoff, not a market quote for any given day.

Multiple years of real weather, computed instantly

Runs the selected crop at the current nitrogen rate across a chosen span of real Rock Springs weather — anywhere from a couple of years to the full 1980–2016 record this engine was validated against, computed client-side in a couple of seconds instead of a Colab session.

Simulate a rotation: corn → winter cover crop → soybean

A more realistic sequence than a single crop in a single year — corn one year, a winter cover crop planted right after harvest and terminated the following spring, then soybean. Evaluated on nitrogen leaching, not just yield, since keeping nitrogen out of the watershed is the point of planting a cover crop at all.

Each phase runs independently rather than carrying real soil state between seasons, so "nitrogen available to the cover crop" is a number you set, not one the model derives. The cover crop uses winter wheat's real parameters, the only fall-planted crop this engine currently models.
