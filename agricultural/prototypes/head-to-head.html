Agricultural Unit · Head-to-head

Check this engine against native Cycles on your own machine

Pick a site and a scenario. This page writes a set of Cycles input files; you run your own copy of Cycles on them. Nothing from Cycles (binary, crop file or output) is bundled here, and nothing you load leaves your browser. Step 1 of 3 is built: the exporter. Reading your Cycles output back in and overlaying this engine on it comes next.

1. Site

Real STATSGO2 soil and real NLDAS-2 weather, resolved to the nearest ~55 km cell. Pick a preset or upload a KML point or polygon for any location in the CONUS.

2. Scenario

Continuous corn (CornRM.90), one broadcast UAN application and a planter pass on day 110, planting between day 110 and 131 once soil temperature is above 12 °C: the scenario the 16-site accuracy table is built on.
