Check this engine against native Cycles on your own machine

This page lets you check our from-scratch reimplementation of Cycles against your own copy of Cycles, on identical inputs. You pick a site (or load your own weather and soil files), download a set of Cycles input files, run Cycles yourself, and drop its output back here. The page then runs our engine on the same inputs in your browser and shows where the two agree and where they do not. Nothing from Cycles (binary, crop file or output) is bundled here, and nothing you load leaves your browser.

Before you start

You need a Cycles v1.4.4 release on your machine (the binary and its input/ folder with GenericCrops.crop). Version 1.5.x writes different output files and is not read by this page yet.

Where this engine is weak. We would rather you read this first than discover it:

  • Fertilized corn (150 kg N/ha) across 16 sites: typical yearly grain error about 1.1 Mg/ha (about 13%), year-to-year correlation about 0.82. Unfertilized corn is much weaker (correlation about 0.3, yield level too high).
  • Nitrogen losses (leaching, denitrification, N2O, volatilization) are typically off by a factor of about 3. Soil mineral nitrogen runs high at organic-matter-rich sites.
  • Soybean is the continuous no-fertilizer case only; the engine runs about 14% low at Rock Springs and high at semi-arid sites. Wheat, silage corn and pasture are not offered because they do not meet our own accuracy bar.
  • Water-limited sites (Kansas, Texas, Arkansas) match best; some sandy and humid sites run 5 to 15% low in dry years.
  • The engine is a simplification: no full six-pool soil system by layer for every process, no topography beyond the slope you supply, and several formulas were fitted to Cycles output because the published equations omit their constants.

The full accuracy statement and the list of mechanisms we tried and rejected are in ACCURACY.md and QUESTIONS_FOR_DEVS.md.

Cycles already installed? Unzip the step 2 download into your Cycles v1.4.4 folder and run run_cycles.bat (Windows) or bash run_cycles.sh (Mac/Linux). It runs Cycles and makes a *_output.zip. How to read these numbers

Engine / Cycles is the ratio of the two means over the common years: 1.00 means the engine matches Cycles on average, 0.90 means it runs 10% low.

Mean abs error is the average size of the yearly miss, in the quantity's own units, regardless of direction.

Correlation says whether the engine ranks the years the way Cycles does (good years and bad years), independent of level. A model can have the right average and the wrong years, or the reverse; read both. Values near 0 mean the year-to-year pattern is not captured.

Planting day is a check on the temperature-and-moisture rule, not on crop growth. Nitrogen fluxes are annual totals (kg N/ha) and are the engine's weakest outputs.
