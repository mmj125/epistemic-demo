Why Triangles

For a joint to stay put, every push and pull on it — from the members connected to it and any load — has to cancel out exactly. That's Newton's first law: no net force, no motion. A triangle gives every joint enough directions of support to always find that balance. Some other shapes don't. Click members on and off below and watch what happens.
