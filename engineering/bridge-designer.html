Truss Bridge Designer

Pick a module scale and a number of layers below, then generate the full candidate "ground structure" for that configuration — every member the grid could possibly have, all present at once. From there, click any member or node to remove it (click again to put it back) to cut cost and mass while keeping the bridge stable enough to hold a 1kg mass at the center of the 130cm span. The truss extends one extra bay past each support onto the abutment — real bridges need to overlap the abutment, not just touch it at a single point. Want fewer bays of bracing on just one row? Remove that row's members over the bays you don't want — you don't need to regenerate anything to do that, just click them off. Try a small scale and watch how many members it takes; try one layer and see if it's stiff enough before adding a second.

The side panel now tracks a real bill of materials (rods and connectors, by color and count) and an iteration log — click "Run Simulation" any time to score your current design and compare it against your last one, even after switching to a completely different scale or layer count.

Click any member or node below to remove it — click it again to put it back. This is how you trim the full grid down to a real design, including making just one row shorter than the rest.
