Truss Bridge Designer

The full candidate "ground structure" for an X-large-scale bridge (red 6.5cm legs, purple 9.5cm diagonals) — 20 bays, exactly spanning the required 130cm. Remove members to cut cost and mass while keeping the bridge stable enough to hold a 1kg mass at the center of the span without excessive deflection.

This first version is one fixed scale and depth so the core interactive grid can be checked before the rest is layered on — real cost/mass numbers, other module scales, adjustable depth, and the iteration log are not built yet (see the note in the side panel).
