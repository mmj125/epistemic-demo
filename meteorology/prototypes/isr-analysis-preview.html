Is the sun changing?

You're looking at 26 years of NASA satellite measurements of incoming solar radiation — the energy Earth receives from the sun at the top of the atmosphere. Read the chart, then answer what it actually shows.

This isn't building toward a verdict on whether climate change is real — that's not what one chart does, in either direction. It's one measurement. Learn to read it carefully, and pay attention to what it rules out and what it still leaves open. You'll get other data displays and a chance to pursue your own question later in this investigation.
